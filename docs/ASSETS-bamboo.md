# Bambus-Tempel kit (track 2)

All props are original, generated by `tools/blender/props/props_bamboo.py` with the shared toolkit (`kit.py`),
same conventions as `docs/ASSETS-props.md`: metres, origin on the ground, front = +Z, vertex colours with baked
AO, one material per prop (plus an `_emit` material on lanterns), Draco. No new textures or HDRIs: the theme
reuses the Kleeblatt sets (`grass dirt rock planks wood`) and `meadow_day` for image lighting; the visible
sky, fog and mood come from the track's `EnvironmentDef` (procedural sky) and the theme.

Total size of the 21 files: **0.36 MB**.

**`bamboo_temple`** (structure model on branch `temple`). Front (+Z) faces the entry. Clear lane |x| < 4.6 m,
5.6 m high through both gates; courtyard walls with inner faces at x = +-5.8 (the structure collider uses
width 12). Children: `door_l` (+X) / `door_r` (-X), hinges at x = +-4.6 on the entry face, opened by the
standard barn logic (`door_l.rotation.y = +angle`); `gong` (pivot on its top bar at x = -5.2, on the driver's
left, 7 m past the centre, 3.3 m up) swung by the theme about its local Z when the sim rings it. The lantern
beam over the lane is 3 m before the centre, hook at 7.4 m.

## Built in code (theme `bamboo`, `src/track/view/themes/bamboo/`)

- **Bridge** (`bridge.ts`): 450 instanced planks in 6 x 3 cells (one draw call), log stringers, cross beams,
  catenary rope rails and hangers (one merged mesh), glowing warning tape (instanced) on warned / broken cells.
  Warned planks rattle and glow orange; broken ones tumble into the gorge.
- **Bamboo fences** replace the farm fence / rail / boundary visuals (instanced canes); stone walls get a mossy
  triplanar material.
- **Waterfall** at the head of the gorge (found from the terrain), animated sheet + foam + splash vfx.
- **Distant forest**: `bamboo_clump.lod1` instanced on the backdrop ring (700 / 420 / 130 by tier).
- **Materials** (`materials.ts`): forest floor (leaf litter, moss, rock strata on cliffs, raked gravel), worn
  flagstone road (worley joints with moss), all with a `wet` uniform (darker, glossier, puddles) that the
  rain animates from lap 2.
