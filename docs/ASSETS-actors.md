# Actors assets: drivers, karts, items

Owner: actors team. Everything here is generated by Python scripts in `tools/blender/actors/` (Blender 5.2, headless);
nothing is modelled by hand. Contact sheet for review: `docs/actors-sheet.webp`.

## Regenerate

```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe" -b -P tools/blender/actors/build_all.py
```

### How the toolkit works

- `sdf.py`: signed-distance primitives (ellipsoid, round cone, rounded box, torus, cylinder) combined with smooth union /
  subtraction, sampled on a grid and meshed with surface nets. The same smooth blending gives soft vertex colours and
  soft skin weights (every primitive carries a colour and a bone). Paint volumes add markings; SDF ambient occlusion is
  baked into the colours.
- `anatomy.py`: the shared seated body (pear body, stubby legs, arms solved by two-bone IK onto the steering-wheel
  grips, round head), eye / mouth parts with morph shapes, face projection helpers.
- `characters.py` (otter + helpers) and `characters2.py` (the other seven): one function per animal.
- `rig.py`: one armature layout for all drivers, skinning from the SDF weights, and all clips written as code
  (rotations in armature axes relative to the seated rest pose, arms re-solved with analytic IK per frame), so every clip
  is retargeted to each character's proportions automatically.
- `karts.py` / `build_kart.py`: the shared kart platform as SDF, themed parts per character, lathe-built tyres with real
  tread blocks. Kart bodies and items get an analytic texture bake (`common.bake_texture`): every texel's 3D position
  is found by rasterising the triangles in UV space and the paint SDFs are evaluated there, so markings stay crisp at
  low triangle counts (no Cycles needed).

## Axes and units

glTF: metres, Y up, front = +Z, +X = left (exported from Blender where front = -Y). Origin of a kart = ground under the
kart centre. Origin of a driver = the seat point (put it on the kart's `seat` node with identity transform).

## Karts `kart.<id>.glb`

Nodes (children of the root node `kart`):

| Node | Position (glTF) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `body` | 0,0,0 | chassis, seat, bumpers, exhausts, themed parts. Material `body_paint` (512 px WebP texture, clearcoat) |
| `wheel_fl` | 0.52, 0.22, 0.66 | origin at the wheel centre, radius 0.22, width 0.20. Spin about local X, steer about local Y |
| `wheel_fr` | -0.52, 0.22, 0.66 | mirrored geometry (rim faces outwards) |
| `wheel_rl` | 0.52, 0.255, -0.60 | radius 0.255, width 0.25 |
| `wheel_rr` | -0.52, 0.255, -0.60 | |
| `steering` | 0, 0.66, 0.14 | steering wheel; its local +Y is the column axis (tilted 28° towards the driver). Rotating about local Y turns it; **negative = right turn**. Full lock used by the clips: 0.75 rad |
| `seat` | 0, 0.30, -0.22 | empty: driver root goes here |
| `exhaust_l`, `exhaust_r` | ±0.20, 0.56, -0.95 | empties at the pipe tips (for flames / smoke) |

Overall: 1.95 m long (bumper to bumper), 1.29 m wide over the tyres, body top about 0.6 m; with driver about 1.4 m tall.
About 12 k triangles, 110–120 KB each. Draw calls: 6 (body, steering, 4 wheels).

Themes: Fauli green with leaves and a leaf spoiler; Wolke sky blue with cloud puffs and a bell; Rubi red-orange with
flame stripes, rear wing and striped antenna; Stupsi plum with spiked bumper, apple and mushrooms; Pingo blue/white with
snow caps, icicles, fish and fins; Kapi orange with citrus slices, onsen tub rim and leaf spoiler; Otti teal with waves,
shell and life ring; Axi pink/lilac with feathery gill fins and bubbles.

## Drivers `char.<id>.glb`

Ids: `sloth sheep redpanda hedgehog penguin capybara otter axolotl`. 11–15 k triangles, 270–350 KB, no textures.

Meshes (all skinned to the armature `rig`):

| Mesh | Material name | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `<id>_fur` | `fur` (axolotl: `skin`) | body, head, ears, tail; vertex colours (markings, blush, baked AO) |
| `<id>_face` | `gloss` | eyes, highlights, nose, mouth, whiskers, claws, beak. Morph targets `blink`, `happy`, `hurt` |
| `<id>_acc` | `cloth` | signature accessory (leaf, scarf, yuzu, starfish, apple, ribbon + bell); none for Rubi |

Bones (same names for every driver): `root hips spine chest neck head ear_l ear_r upperarm_l forearm_l hand_l
upperarm_r forearm_r hand_r thigh_l thigh_r` plus `tail_1..tail_3` where there is a tail (sheep, penguin, capybara: `tail_1` only;
sloth and hedgehog none). The axolotl's `ear_*` bones drive its top gills.

Clips (30 fps, sampled; loop = authored seamless):

| Clip | Length | Loop | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `idle` | 2.0 s | yes | breathing, head bob, ear twitch, tail sway, hands on wheel |
| `steer_l` / `steer_r` | 1.0 s | yes | lean into the turn, head looks into it, wheel at full lock (hands follow) |
| `drift_l` / `drift_r` | 1.0 s | yes | stronger lean, bounce, ears flapping, tail streaming |
| `boost` | 0.6 s | yes | lean forward, ears back, tail back, vibration |
| `hit` | 1.2 s | no | dizzy wobble, arms flail off the wheel |
| `squash` | 1.0 s | no | squashed down, arms out, ears flat |
| `cheer` | 1.2 s | no | fist pump with the right arm, bounce |
| `look_back` | 1.0 s | yes | twist round over the right shoulder, right hand off the wheel |
| `throw` | 0.6 s | no | wind up and throw with the right arm |
| `win` | 2.0 s | yes | both arms up, bouncing |
| `lose` | 2.0 s | yes | slumped, head shaking, ears drooping |

Clips are full poses (not additive); `createKartView` blends them by weight (e.g. `idle` + `steer_r` by steering amount).

## LOD: low-detail opponents

`char.<id>.lod1.glb` and `kart.<id>.lod1.glb` are drop-in replacements for the opponents on phones: about 5 k
triangles per kart + driver instead of about 25 k. Review sheet: `docs/actors-lod-sheet.webp`.

They are rebuilt by the same scripts with `--lod 1` (env `ACTORS_LOD`), not decimated from the GLBs, so colours,
skin weights and morphs are computed on the low mesh. LOD 0 output is byte-identical to before.

| | full | lod1 |
|---|---|---|
| Driver fur | 9–11 k tris | 21 % of that (1.9–2.3 k), same SDF |
| Face (`gloss`) | 3 k | 0.5–0.8 k: spheres and tubes at half the segments; `blink` / `happy` / `hurt` kept |
| Accessory (`cloth`) | 900 | 250 |
| Clips | 30 fps | 15 fps (same names and lengths); rest-pose translation / scale channels dropped, rotations as normalized int16 (`KHR_mesh_quantization`) by `glb_slim.py` |
| Kart body | 4.6 k, 512 px texture | 1.25 k, 256 px texture |
| Steering wheel | 600 | 160 |
| Wheel | 1.7 k, tread blocks, bolts | 272: 16-sided tyre, rim and hub, no tread or bolts |

Identical between full and lod1: node names, hierarchy and transforms (`wheel_*`, `steering`, `seat`, `exhaust_*`),
wheel radius and lowest point, bones and bind pose, clip names and durations, mesh and material names, morph names.
Files: drivers 100–125 KB (most of it is the 13 clips), karts 41–45 KB.

## Items `item.<id>.glb`

`itembox acorn honey giftbox yarn bee bubble carrot goldcarrot lolly cloud`. One mesh named like the item, origin at
its centre, 1.2–1.9 k triangles, 256 px WebP texture, 11–36 KB. Sizes (largest extent): itembox 1.0 m (gift box with
paw prints and ribbon), giftbox 0.85 m (red with gold ribbon), bubble 1.0 m (transparent, material `item_bubble`),
cloud 1.0 m (grumpy thunder cloud with bolt), acorn 0.55 m, honey jar 0.6 m, yarn 0.6 m, carrot / goldcarrot 0.75 m,
lolly 0.9 m, bee 0.35 m (use several for `bees`). Mapping for gameplay ids: `acorn3` → 3 × `item.acorn`,
`bees` → `item.bee`, `carrot3` → 3 × `item.carrot`, `yawn` has no model (it is an effect).

## Portraits

`public/assets/portraits/<id>.webp`: 512 × 512, transparent background, driver in kart, three-quarter front view.

## Runtime (`src/actors/`)

- `createAssets()` – `AssetLibrary` with Draco (`/draco/`), cache, progress, `SkeletonUtils` cloning, placeholders for
  missing files (one warning per missing name), textures downscaled to the tier's `maxTextureSize`.
- `raceAssets(config)` – all drivers + karts on the grid and the item models.
- `createKartView(kart, ctx)` – kart + driver: wheel spin / steer / suspension travel from `kart.wheels[]`, steering
  wheel, body pitch from acceleration and roll from `kart.lean`, squash and stretch on `kart:jump` / `kart:land`,
  flattened on squash, shrink from `KartModifier.scale`, barrel roll on `kart:trick`, visual spin / tumble on hits when the
  physics does not rotate the body itself, animation state machine with cross-fades (steering blend, drift, boost, look
  back, hit / squash / cheer on overtaking (`race:rank`) / throw on `item:use`, win or lose after `race:finish`), blinking
  and `happy` / `hurt` expressions. The root object is named `kartview:<id>` with `userData.kartId`; the kart's
  `exhaust_l` / `exhaust_r` empties are inside it for effects.
- Fur: `furMaterial(true)` (TSL sheen + fibre noise + rim fuzz) when `quality.fur`, plain otherwise; on tier `high` the
  human player's driver also gets 5 alpha-tested fur shells (`addFurShells`).
